Tao Te Ching · and the nine scales under your feet

Five old lines that turn out to be literally true about matter.

Not metaphor. Each of these was written two and a half thousand years before anyone could weigh an atom, and each one describes something that was later measured — the emptiness inside a stone, the place where fusion stops paying, the exact size below which looking no longer works. The instruments below are the real arithmetic. Put your hand on them.

Drag · slide · click. Every panel is live.

一
鑿戶牖以為室，當其無，有室之用。
Cut doors and windows to make a room; it is the emptiness that makes it useful.
Tao Te Ching, 11

That is an iron atom. Twenty-six electrons, and a nucleus holding all but a four-thousandth of the weight. Magnify until the cloud leaves the frame and there is nothing — and nothing — and then a single point, nine femtometres across.

The volume ratio is one part in 3.9×10¹³. Every picture you have ever seen of an atom draws that nucleus about three thousand times too wide, because drawn honestly it is smaller than a pixel. You are not mostly water. You are mostly nothing, arranged very carefully.

二
人法地，地法天，天法道，道法自然。
Earth follows heaven, heaven follows the Way, and the Way follows what is so of itself.
Tao Te Ching, 25
drag the dark disc across the sun

Nothing here is scheduled. There is no eclipse event and no trigger. There are two circles with real angular radii, and the light is whatever the overlap leaves — so the sky dims because the sun is covered, and not the other way round.

The fall is not linear, and that is the part worth feeling. A star is limb darkened: bright in the middle, dim at the rim. The first bite costs less light than its area and the last sliver costs far more, which is why an eclipse seems to do almost nothing for an hour and then everything in ninety seconds. 道法自然 — it follows from what it is.

三
反者道之動。
Returning is the motion of the Way.
Tao Te Ching, 40

Every element is on this curve, and the curve turns. To the left, joining two nuclei gives energy back — that is what a star is, and it is the only reason the sky has anything in it. To the right, splitting gives energy back. At the top, neither does.

The peak sits at A = 58, which is iron. A star that has fused its way up to iron has arrived somewhere with no downhill left in any direction: it can no longer hold itself up, and it falls in — in about one second. That collapse is a supernova, and it is how the iron got out. The return is not a metaphor here; it is the sign of a derivative.

四
道生一，一生二，二生三，三生萬物。
The Way bears one, one bears two, two bears three, and three bears the ten thousand things.
Tao Te Ching, 42
click a step, or let it run

Hydrogen was made in the first three minutes and nothing else was. Everything after it had to be assembled inside something, one step at a time, and the steps are countable: two hydrogens are not enough, three heliums make a carbon, carbon and helium make oxygen, and from there the shells of a dying star do the rest until they reach iron and stop.

The oxygen you are breathing was made in the helium-burning shell of a star more than eight times the mass of the sun, and thrown out when that star collapsed. The iron in your blood is mostly from white dwarfs detonating. 三生萬物 — and it took about nine billion years.

五
視之不見，名曰夷。
Look at it and you do not see it: call it the featureless.
Tao Te Ching, 14

Green light is five hundred and fifty nanometres from crest to crest, and no arrangement of glass will ever resolve much below half of that. It is not a engineering problem. It is the size of the ruler.

Slide past that mark and the panel stops being able to show you anything, because that is what happens. Two of the nine scales — the crystal and the atom — are on the far side of it. Everything anyone knows about them was inferred from how things scatter, never seen. 視之不見: look at it and you do not see it. The line was written about the Way. It is also the specification of a microscope.
